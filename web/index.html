How wrong are the first reserve estimates?

Every year the operator re-estimates how much oil and gas a field will ever produce. We followed that number for Norwegian fields through annual resource accounts (), and asked which fields end up far from where they started.

About the UK. The task was suggested for the UK sector. The regulator publishes sector totals only, so a year-by-year estimate per UK field is not open today. We found it for four years: the government's annual “Brown Book” was published on the web from 1998 to 2001 with the operator's estimate for every field, and those editions survive in the Internet Archive. Together with start-up estimates from a 2000 MIT paper and production data from the regulator, that is enough to test the UK too. Norway publishes the full table for 2004–2025, so the main analysis is Norwegian.
